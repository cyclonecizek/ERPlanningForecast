NWS marine forecasts along the way

▸Guidance sources

Wind, gusts and weather blend the NWS forecast and the models; seas, swell and wind waves blend the wave models, the NWS waves and the NBM wave height. Weights apply wherever a source has a value; untick a source to leave it out. The ensembles set the chances of strong wind.

Wind in knots at 10 m. Seas are the significant wave height (the average of the highest third of the waves); with swell and wind waves they are given as a range between two percentiles of the guidance, set above. Chances of wind come from the GEFS (31 members) and ECMWF (51 members) ensembles, moved by any edit; chances of seas come from the spread of the wave models plus the usual forecast error at that lead time. Rows between waypoints are on a straight track at the set speed.

Sources: NWS gridded forecast and marine zone forecasts (api.weather.gov), models from Open-Meteo (NBM, GFS, ECMWF, ICON, GEM, UKMO and the GEFS and ECMWF ensembles), wave models from Open-Meteo marine (NOAA WAVEWATCH III, ECMWF WAM, Météo-France MFWAM, DWD GWAM), the NBM wave height from the NBM text bulletins (Iowa Environmental Mesonet), and the coastline from Natural Earth.
